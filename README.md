# Hill Street

## Overview

Let's imagine a world of urban agriculture, synthetic biology and mixed reality. [Vertical / Container farming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertical_farming) is failing to deliver on promises.  One criticism is the systems rush to automate processes that could be easily done manually, in a sort of euphoria of unneccesary technology.

Suppose we took an urban parking lot and tried to turn it into a food garden a' la the "Urban Permaculture" of Kev Polk at Edenicty.  Perhaps a clever blend of bioremediation, AI and botany can bring efficient land use.

* Why use flourescent lighting to grow crops during the day?
* Current farming practices waste water and resources.
* Do we need to create more farmland from the desert? Or can we use this land for biodiversity or wildlife preserves?
* Use urban infrastructure to best advantage. Water and transport should be very easy.
